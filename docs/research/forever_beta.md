# WoW Forever (Project Epoch / Camelot 1.60.x) — Beta Research

Compiled from external sources (wowtbc.gg class guides, icy-veins wow-forever overviews). All content below is **beta/preview** information; the client is not yet live (release cited as Nov 4, 2026 by leprestore; icy-veins countdown ~18 days at scrape time). Treat every rotation as provisional — guides repeatedly state "The sims will eventually tell us what the optimal rotation is."

Sources:
- wowtbc.gg warcraftforever class guides: https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/...
- icy-veins wow-forever overviews: https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/...
- wowtbc.gg "What We Know": https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/news/what-we-know/

## Cross-class notes

- New race: Skyborne (Alliance/Horde variants). New race/class combos include Human Hunter, Undead Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, etc.
- Quality-of-life shared: traps usable in combat (Hunter), pets scale with stats, Auto Shot no longer clipped by abilities, Judgement no longer consumes Seals (Paladin), blessings 1 hour, BoK baseline, Omen of Clarity baseline (Druid), Entangling Roots works indoors, Swiftmend no longer consumes hots, Ghost Wolf instant + usable indoors (Shaman), rage normalized around weapon speed (Warrior), Tactical Mastery baseline (Warrior), dots can crit (all specs).
- Guides are tagged "Level 30 (Beta)" and "Leveling 1-60"; level-60 endgame rotations are NOT published — only beta-level rotations exist. **Unknown:** final level-60 tuning, itemization, raid mechanics.

## Hunter

Custom spells (wowtbc.gg + icy-veins):
- Summon Hawk (BM capstone-ish; shares CD with Arcane Shot; max 2 hawks + pet) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/beast-mastery-hunter/
- Sniper Shot (MM; 4s cast, massive shot) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/marksmanship-hunter/
- Strider Kick (Survival melee) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/survival-hunter/
- Lone Wolf (MM early talent; +20% damage with no pet) — icy-veins hunter overview
- Mine! (listed on landing page) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/news/what-we-know/ (beta, unconfirmed in guides)
- Aimed Shot now baseline sharing CD with Multi-Shot; Aspect of the Beast for melee AP.

Rotations (beta, provisional):
- BM ST: Aspect of the Hawk → Trueshot Aura → Hunter's Mark → Rapid Fire/Bestial Wrath/on-use → Intimidation → Serpent Sting → Summon Hawk if <2 active → Aimed Shot → Auto Shot. (wowtbc.gg BM guide)
- MM ST: Aspect of the Hawk → Trueshot Aura → Hunter's Mark → Rapid Fire + on-use → Serpent Sting → Sniper Shot → Aimed Shot → Auto Shot. (wowtbc.gg MM guide)
- Survival (melee): Aspect of the Beast → Hunter's Mark → Rapid Fire + on-use → Mongoose Bite with Expose Prey procs → Immolation Trap → Strider Kick → Raptor Strike. (wowtbc.gg Survival guide)

Unknown: final sims; whether Sniper Shot is a PvE gain (icy-veins flags possible damage loss due to 4s cast); world-buff raid restrictions unconfirmed.

## Warrior

Custom spells:
- Spearing Strike (Arms; vs Dragonkin/Giants) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/arms-warrior/
- Victory Rush — arms/prot/fury guides
- Bloodthrill (Overpower procs) — arms guide
- Tactical Mastery now baseline; Improved Slam moved to Arms tree; Vanguard (Charge in Defensive Stance); rage normalized around weapon speed.

Rotations (beta, provisional):
- Arms ST: Berserker Stance (swap to Battle for Rend/Overpower/Spearing Strike) → Battle Shout → Recklessness + on-use (execute phase) → Bloodrage → Sunder if <5 → Execute <20% → Rend → Heroic Strike excess rage → Spearing Strike vs Dragonkin/Giants → Overpower (Bloodthrill procs or dodge) → Mortal Strike → Whirlwind → Slam. (wowtbc.gg Arms guide)
- Fury ST: Berserker Stance → Battle Shout → Recklessness/Death Wish + on-use → Bloodrage → Sunder if <5 → Execute <20% → Heroic Strike + Hamstring excess rage → Bloodthirst → Whirlwind. (wowtbc.gg Fury guide)
- Prot ST: Defensive Stance → Battle Shout → Shield Wall/Last Stand CDs → Bloodrage → 5 Sunder → Thunder Clap → Demoralizing Shout → Shield Block → Heroic Strike excess → Shield Slam → Revenge → Sunder. Charge/Thunder Clap usable in Defensive Stance. (wowtbc.gg Prot guide)

Unknown: sims; optimal stance-dance timing; whether Bloodthrill proc rate changes.

## Paladin

Custom spells:
- Twist of Light (Seal Twisting enabler) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/retribution-paladin/
- Holy Strike (Ret/Prot/Holy) — retribution/prot/holy guides
- Light's Vigil (Holy AoE heal) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/holy-paladin/
- Seal of Fury (Prot) — https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/protection-paladin/
- Templar's Bulwark (Prot emergency CD), Swift Judgement (taunt reset), Holy Shock, Champion of the Light (AP+Spell Damage scaling).
- Judgement no longer consumes Seals; BoK baseline; blessings 1 hour.

Rotations (beta, provisional):
- Ret ST: assigned Aura → assigned Blessing/BoK → Seal of Crusader Judgement debuff (refreshed by Sacred Arbiter) → alternate Seal of Command / Seal of Righteousness after auto attacks for Twist of Light → Holy Strike → Judgement → Hammer of Wrath <20% → Exorcism (Undead/Demon) → Consecration. (wowtbc.gg Ret guide)
- Holy priority: assigned Aura → assigned Blessing/BoK → maintain Seal of Light/Wisdom Judgement debuff → Divine Favor + Holy Shock/Holy Light for guaranteed crit → Light's Vigil then Holy Shock x2 for AoE → Holy Light with Infusion of Light procs → Holy Shock instant → Flash of Light/Holy Light. Downranking unknown. (wowtbc.gg Holy guide)
- Prot ST: Righteous Fury → Aura → Blessing → maintain Seal of Fury (Seal of Righteousness when not tanking) → Holy Shield → Templar's Bulwark CD → Swift Judgement + Judgement (emergency taunt/snap threat) → Holy Strike → Judgement (taunt with Seal of Fury) → Consecration → Exorcism (Undead/Demon). (wowtbc.gg Prot guide)

Unknown: downranking heal behavior (Holy); optimal seal-twist timing window; sims.

## Druid

Custom spells:
- Revive, Wild Growth, Berserk, Eclipse, Mangle; Omen of Clarity baseline affects spellcasting; Entangling Roots works indoors; Swiftmend no longer consumes hots.
- Feral DPS: Shifting Power (power-shifting energy gen), Berserk; party-wide 3% crit (exclusive with Moonkin Form).
- Feral Tank: Primal Bite, Lacerate, Berserk, Frenzied Regeneration (real defensive CD).
- Balance: Eclipse.

Rotations (beta, provisional):
- Balance ST: Moonkin Form → maintain Moonfire + Insect Swarm → Starfire with Eclipse stacks → Wrath. (wowtbc.gg Balance guide)
- Feral DPS ST: maintain Faerie Fire (Feral) → Berserk + on-use → Shred with Omen of Clarity procs → Shifting Power if <60 energy → Rip at 5 CP → Rake → Shred → Ferocious Bite if won't delay Rip. (wowtbc.gg Feral DPS guide)
- Feral Tank ST: Dire Bear Form → Frenzied Regeneration CD → Berserk + on-use → 5 Lacerate → Faerie Fire (Feral) → Demoralizing Roar → Maul excess rage → Primal Bite → Lacerate if <5 → Swipe. (wowtbc.gg Feral Tank guide)

Unknown: sims; Shifting Power energy values; whether Feral DPS power-shifting is mana-gated.

## Shaman

Custom spells:
- Totemic Projection, Lava Burst, Call of the Elements (drops all 4 totems), Improved Ghost Wolf (instant), Rage of the Farseer (personal Bloodlust for Enhance), Riptide. Dots can crit.

Rotations (beta, provisional):
- Elemental ST: maintain Searing/Mana Spring/assigned Earth+Air totems via Call of the Elements → Elemental Mastery + on-use → Flame Shock → Lava Burst if Flame Shock on target → Chain Lightning → Lightning Bolt → Flame Shock while moving. Mana for Chain Lightning on ST unclear. (wowtbc.gg Elemental guide)
- Enhancement ST: Windfury Weapon → maintain Strength of Earth/Mana Spring/Searing + Windfury or Grace of Air via Call of the Elements → Rage of the Farseer + on-use → Lightning Bolt at 5 Maelstrom Weapon → Stormstrike → Flame Shock or Earth Shock (Earth Shock if Flame Shock DoT present) → Fire Nova. (wowtbc.gg Enhancement guide)

Unknown: sims; mana sustainability for Elemental Chain Lightning; Maelstrom Weapon stack behavior.
